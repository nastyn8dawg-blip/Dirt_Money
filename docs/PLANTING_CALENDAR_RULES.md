# Planting Calendar Rules

## Goal

Planting windows prevent late leased land or post-harvest fields from bypassing seasonal crop realism.

## Crop Windows

- Corn: normal in Early Spring and Late Spring; late/reduced yield in Early Summer; closed after that.
- Soybeans: normal in Late Spring and Early Summer; late/reduced yield in Mid Summer; closed after that.
- Hay: normal through Mid Summer; late/reduced yield through Early Fall; closed after that.
- Cover Crop: available as a soil-building option when ground is open.
- Winter Wheat: currently flexible to preserve the existing early cash-crop path.
- Fallow: always available when ground is open.

## Leased Land

Newly leased fields use the same `plantingWindowStatus` rules as starting fields. Late Hollis Back 20 cannot plant full-season soybeans or corn after their windows close.

## UI Rules

Closed windows show plain disabled reasons such as "Too late to plant corn this year" or "Soybean planting window has passed." Late allowed planting labels the reduced yield potential.
