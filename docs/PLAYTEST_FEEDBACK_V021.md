# Dirt Money v0.2.1 Playtest Feedback

This pass responds to the second friend playtest after the v0.2 engagement update. The goal is not more scope; it is clearer rules, fewer exploits, and better player guidance.

## Critical Bugs

- Soil Test felt broken because it charged money but did not leave a visible result.
- Soil Test and other field inputs could be repeated without enough feedback.
- Game ending/year ending was unclear.

## Exploits

- Fertilize and Treat Weeds could be used late to inflate harvest value.
- Repeated NPC clicks, especially Marge-style interactions, could farm benefits.
- Field inputs needed same-stage limits to prevent repeated stacking.

## Unclear UI/Feedback

- Soil Test needed a result panel, recommendation, and current/stale status.
- Contract cards needed a clear next step and failure reason.
- Players needed more direct guidance on what to do this week.

## Field Action Rule Problems

- Spraying weeds increased crop stress, which violated player expectation.
- Ready crops needed yield lock rules.
- Fertilizer, spray, scouting, soil testing, and fallow needed repeat limits and disabled reasons.

## Contract Clarity Problems

- Marge's Co-op Seed Delivery did not explain what action was required.
- Accepted and failing contracts needed next-step, deadline, location, consequence, and status text.

## Progression Gaps

- Players expected equipment purchases.
- Added a first-pass equipment upgrade layer instead of a full dealership.

## Calendar/Season Issues

- Players wanted to know the time of year.
- The game needed a visible Year / Week / Season label and an end-of-year transition.

## Future Content Ideas

- More dynamic events, especially weather, county gossip, bank pressure, and equipment warnings.
- More seasons and richer year-to-year consequences.
- A deeper equipment dealership and land market later.
