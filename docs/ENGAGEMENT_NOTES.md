# Dirt Money Engagement Notes

## Design Goal
Dirt Money should feel like a living rural operation. The player is still making farm-management decisions, but the county now interrupts with calls, weather, warnings, and small opportunities.

## Weekly Event Layer
- Each week generates 1-3 visible items in "This Week in Ash Creek."
- Events can be neighbor calls, weather notices, market shifts, bank pressure, equipment warnings, salvage leads, or reputation opportunities.
- Events expire after the week unless resolved.
- Choice events log their consequences immediately and show a result card in the UI.

## Result Feedback
Important actions should surface a short result card:
- Soil and scout results.
- Contract accepted, work started, completed, failed, or abandoned.
- Harvest, repair, cash, credit, reputation, and weather outcomes.
- End-of-year result.

## Contract Engagement
Contracts now move through:
1. Available.
2. Accepted / Action Needed.
3. In Progress.
4. Ready to Complete.
5. Completed, failed, abandoned, or archived.

The player must perform the active work step before a contract progresses. The board should always show what happens if ignored.

## Field Management Engagement
The Fields overview is the player's weekly operations screen:
- Read all fields at once.
- See basic observations without paying.
- Use batch actions when the answer is obvious.
- Open field detail for deeper calls.

## Reputation Engagement
Reputation now changes the county texture:
- Low reputation adds bank pressure and fewer easy favors.
- Normal reputation keeps ordinary work flowing.
- High reputation creates better tips and land/equipment opportunities.
- 90+ reputation gets explicit county acknowledgement.
