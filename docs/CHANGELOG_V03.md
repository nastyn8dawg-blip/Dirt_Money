# Dirt Money v0.3 Changelog

## Added
- Weekly "This Week in Ash Creek" event inbox with calls, weather notices, market leads, and reputation opportunities.
- Choice-driven weekly events with cash, reputation, field, equipment, market, and contract-board effects.
- Result cards for major action feedback.
- Active contract work steps, including Marge's "Haul Seed" action.
- Contract abandonment with reputation consequences.
- Fields overview with crop, stage, stress, weeds, fertility, soil test status, scout status, recommendations, warnings, and quick actions.
- Batch field actions: Scout All, Soil Test All Unchecked, Treat High Weed Fields, Fertilize Recommended Fields, and Harvest All Ready Fields.
- Basic field observations plus paid scout reports.
- Stress cause/effect/recovery explanations.
- High reputation acknowledgement and opportunity events.
- End-of-season outcome classification: Strong, Stable, Struggling, and Bad.
- Additional WebAudio placeholder cues for contract, cash, credit, storm, repair, reputation, and popup feedback.

## Changed
- Accepted contracts now require an explicit active work step before they progress.
- Weekly reports now include event inbox and expired/handled event notes.
- Dashboard priorities now pull from active events, urgent contract steps, field needs, equipment risk, debt pressure, and reputation opportunities.
- Ready crop yield remains locked; late inputs explain why they no longer help.
- Year rollover starts with a clearer opening ledger.

## Fixed
- End-of-year report tone now matches actual cash, debt, reputation, and equipment condition.
- Week 36 report flow now avoids the wrong "Start Week 36" prompt.
- Marge's seed delivery now tells the player exactly what to do and offers a direct Haul Seed action.
