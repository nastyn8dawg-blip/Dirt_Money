# Dirt Money Ag Realism Rules

## Annual Crop Rules
- Corn and soybeans are annual cash crops.
- A field can plant only one corn or soybean crop per year.
- After corn or soybean harvest, the field enters post-harvest status.
- Post-harvest exits are cover crop, fallow/rest, hay establishment, or waiting for next year.
- Year rollover clears annual planting restrictions but keeps previous crop and rotation history.

## Hay Exception
- Hay is allowed to provide multiple smaller cuttings in a year.
- Harvested hay regrows if enough season remains.
- Hay still wears equipment and pays less per cutting than a major grain harvest.

## Fertilizer Limits
- Corn can receive up to two meaningful fertilizer applications per crop cycle.
- Soybeans can receive one meaningful fertilizer application.
- Winter wheat can receive one meaningful application.
- Hay can receive limited fertility support across the year.
- Cover crop is normally not fertilized.
- Ready crops cannot gain yield from fertilizer.

## Scouting Rules
- Scout Field is free.
- Scout All Fields is free.
- Scouting is limited to once per field per week.
- Scouting reveals weed pressure, stress cause, recovery advice, and harvest timing risk.

## Weed Timing
- Weed treatment matters most in early crop stages.
- Corn and soybeans lose more yield from weeds before canopy.
- Once the crop is mature or ready, spraying is disabled because yield impact is mostly set.

## Stress Rules
- Stress must have a cause, effect, and recovery path.
- Drought stress recovers from rain or long-term soil health, not fertilizer spam.
- Wet stress recovers from dry weather, drainage, or waiting, not fertilizer.
- Fertility stress can recover from timely fertilizer, cover crop, or rotation.
- Weed stress can recover from timely weed treatment.
- Rotation stress improves next year through crop rotation, hay, fallow, or cover crop.
- Ready crop stress becomes mostly locked into yield, so harvest timing matters more than new inputs.

## Rotation Bonuses
- Corn after corn increases fertility need, stress risk, and lowers yield ceiling.
- Soybeans after corn improves rotation outlook.
- Cover crop and hay improve next-year soil resilience.
- Old School Farmer receives clearer rotation warnings and a lighter repeat-corn penalty.

## Money Sources After Annual Crop Limits
- Hay cuttings.
- Active contracts and custom work.
- Salvage buying, stripping, repair, and flipping.
- Repair advantages for the Mechanic background.
- High-reputation land lease and equipment opportunities.
- Market timing and storage once grain is harvested.
